#   [1. 总述](https://conf.yasdb.com/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=138545264#1-%E6%80%BB%E8%BF%B0)  

yasldr导入数据时，获取分区边界值需要和服务端一样，通过查询系统视图后解码，而不是通过原始定义自己解析。

##   [2. ](https://conf.yasdb.com/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=138545264#4-%E7%89%B9%E6%80%A7)    功能列表

1、新增系统视图，供客户端查询使用

2、解码新增的视图字段，解析出分区边界值

## 4.1 现状分析

现在通过执行如下查询

    SELECT PARTITION_POSITION, HIGH_VALUE FROM ALL_TAB_PARTITIONS WHERE   TABLE_NAME = :1 AND TABLE_OWNER = :2 ORDER BY PARTITION_POSITION

从ALL_TAB_PARTITIONS中获取到分区边界值定义的原始字符串，然后自己解析成分区边界，在导入时和数据值比较后确定数据所属分区

## 4.2 方案设计

1、新增视图IMU_ALL_TAB_PARTITIONS和IMU_ALL_TAB_SUBPARTITIONS，供导入用。新增的视图以IMU开头，表示import use。

视图 IMU_ALL_TAB_PARTITIONS

|字段名称|字段含义|视图创建语句|
|---|---|---|
|TABLE_OWNER|表的用户名|CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW IMU_ALL_TAB_PARTITIONS(TABLE_OWNER, TABLE_NAME, PARTITION_NAME, SUBPARTITION_COUNT, HIGH_VALUE_BIN, PARTITION_POSITION) AS     
  SELECT U.NAME, O.NAME,     
  O.SUBNAME,     
  (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM SYS.TABPART$ WHERE BO# = TP.OBJ#),     
  TP.BHIBOUNDVAL, row_number() over (partition by     [u.name](http://u.name)    ,     [o.name](http://o.name)     order by tp.part#)    
  FROM SYS.OBJ$ O, SYS.TAB$ T, SYS.USER$ U, SYS.TABPART$ TP    
  WHERE TP.BO# = T.OBJ# AND TP.OBJ# = O.OBJ# AND O.OWNER# = U.USER#    
  AND (O.OWNER# = userenv('SCHEMAID')    
  OR O.OBJ# IN (SELECT UOR.OBJ# FROM SYS.USER_OBJROLES$ UOR WHERE USER# = userenv('SCHEMAID'))    
  OR check_sys_privilege(O.OWNER#, O.TYPE#) = 'TRUE')    
  /|
|TABLE_NAME|分区所属表名||
|PARTITION_NAME|分区名||
|SUBPARTITION_COUNT|当前分区的子分区个数||
|HIGH_VALUE_BIN|分区边界值二进制||
|PARTITION_POSITION|分区在表内的位置||


视图IMU_ALL_TAB_SUBPARTITIONS

|字段名称|字段含义|视图创建语句|
|---|---|---|
|TABLE_OWNER|表的用户名|CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW IMU_ALL_TAB_SUBPARTITIONS(TABLE_OWNER, TABLE_NAME, PARTITION_NAME, SUBPARTITION_NAME, HIGH_VALUE_BIN,     
  PARTITION_POSITION, SUBPARTITION_POSITION) AS     
  SELECT U.NAME, O.NAME, PO.SUBNAME, O.SUBNAME, SP.BHIBOUNDVAL,    
  dense_rank() over (partition by     [po.name](http://po.name)     order by p.part#),    
  row_number() over (partition by     [u.name](http://u.name)    ,     [po.name](http://po.name)    , po.subname order by sp.part#)    
  FROM SYS.OBJ$ O, SYS.OBJ$ PO, SYS.USER$ U, SYS.TABPART$ P, SYS.TABPART$ SP    
  WHERE SP.BO# = P.OBJ# AND SP.OBJ# = O.OBJ# AND P.OBJ# = PO.OBJ# AND O.OWNER# = U.USER#     
  AND (O.OWNER# = userenv('SCHEMAID')    
  OR O.OBJ# IN (SELECT UOR.OBJ# FROM SYS.USER_OBJROLES$ UOR WHERE USER# = userenv('SCHEMAID'))    
  OR check_sys_privilege(O.OWNER#, O.TYPE#) = 'TRUE')    
  /|
|TABLE_NAME|分区所属表名||
|PARTITION_NAME|分区名||
|SUBPARTITION_NAME|子分区名||
|HIGH_VALUE_BIN|子分区边界值二进制||
|PARTITION_POSITION|  分区在表内的位置||
|SUBPARTITION_POSITION|子分区在表内的位置||
